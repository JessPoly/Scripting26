The People

The Old

Bonesborough is full of diversity. Filled with many people with unique racial, religious, and demonic heritage. By being one of the oldest towns in the Boiling Isles, some people can even trace their family lineage to before the rule of Emperor Belos!

The New

It is also filled with a lot of newcomers, as there is a recent movement to get new people to live nearby. Since its a town full of witches and demons the people here are not always the friendliest, every witch for themselves am I right? But find the right coven with the right group of people and you are sure to fit in.
