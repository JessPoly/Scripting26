Geography

Location


Bonesborough is a one-of-a-kind place to live full of witches, but its most wonderful part is its boiling rain and its engaging wildlife. The fauna and the flora both work together and ensure anyone visiting will keep their wits sharp, as every turn can bring about peril!

Weather


The rain here is like nothing else you will ever experience anywhere else. A steel umbrella is necessary if you are visiting, unless you can withstand 100c rain on your skin. Its right by the sea too, so if its not raining you can trek on down and watch the Selkidomus’ migrate in the fall.
