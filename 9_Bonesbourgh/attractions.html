Attractions

Marketplace


Go downtown to the daily marketplace and look for all kinds of doodads or thingamajigs your heart needs. You can even visit the midnight market for some magical items of a more illicit manner.

Covention


Once a year the town convention center hosts the Covention, a place for covens of all stature to gather and invite growing young witches into their coven. From the large Emperors Coven, all the way down to the Small Cat Coven, they all have a spot for new people.

Library


If knowledge is more your strong suit, the Bonesborough Library has any book you can need. Have a young child? The children section has supervision and age-appropriate books. Need to do some serious research? Gain access to the restricted sections and learn anything there is to know.
