Economy

Covens


The economy of Bonesborough is heavily dependant on the covens that populate the area. As each coven takes care of one aspect of magic, each coven has the job of providing products for the people at large.

The Construction Coven


Oversees all things you need constructed. From houses to graveyards.

The Healing Coven


Helps any witch young or old with any injury. Their top of the line training cant be beat.

The Potion Coven


In need of a specific potion? This covens ability to cook up anything is unparelleled.
