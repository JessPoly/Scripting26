Grace Ashcroft

About 
Grace Ashcroft is one of the main characters from Resident Evil's latest entry, Resident Evil: Requium. The  story follows  Grace as she sent to investigate a suspicous death in the same place her mother was murdered right infront of her eyes. The only issue is that she's not supposed to be sent out into the field at all, and everything turns to the worst when shes kidnapped and brought to a stange facility. With help from a new ally in Leon Kennedy, can she figure out the mystery of her Mothers murder all while getting her butt out of dodge?

Personality
Grace is a very reluctant hero. I doubt she'd even call herself a hero at all! She's so scared all the time and spends the entire game screaming for her life. Like honestly me too girl if i was in that situation. however, thats just how she is when her life is in perral. In the quieter moments we see she's actually much braver than she gives herself credit for. No matter what happens, what enemies she faces, she still gets herself back up and continues on forward. 

Associates
Alyssa Ashcroft: Alyssa Ashcroff is Grace's adoptive mother. She actually was a playable character in one of Resident Evil's earlier games as an investiative journalist. Unfortunetly her death is infact the catalyst for this whole game's story so she doesn't last long. 

Leon Kennedy: Leon is a long standing character in the Resident Evil series and he returns again to be a main character alongside Grace in this game. He plays an important part in the story as he relativly remains stable and acts as a rock for Grace to grow and lean on. 

Emily: Emily is a young blind girl Grace finds locked up while she looks for a way out. Determined to not just leave her to die, Grace takes her along to help her get out of this place

Dr. Gideon: Dr Gideon is the main villain to Grace's story. He's the one who kidnaps her, and is directly involved in the murder of her mother.
